# Contact

**CJW Apps**

Email: [CJW-Apps@outlook.com](mailto:CJW-Apps@outlook.com)

Email is the way to reach us for everything: help with an app, a problem
to report, an idea, or a question before you buy.

## So we can help quickly, please say

- which app it is about: Audiobook Library, eBook AI Reader, eBook Library
  or CJW-Apps ITAM;
- the version, shown in the app under **About**;
- for the book apps, whether you use Android or Windows;
- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead. A
  screenshot helps.

Please don't send passwords, licence keys or recovery keys. We never need
them.

## Business enquiries

For CJW-Apps ITAM (questions about Pro, using it across several sites, or
a demonstration copy) use the same address and put **ITAM** in the
subject.
