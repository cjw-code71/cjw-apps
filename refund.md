# CJW Apps — Refund Policy

*Last updated 26 November 2026*

## 30-day money-back guarantee

If you are not happy with a CJW-Apps ITAM licence key you bought from us,
you can have a **full refund within 30 days** of the payment. You do not
need to give a reason. This applies to new purchases, renewals and
upgrades.

## How to ask for a refund

- Email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) with your
  organisation's name and the order number from Paddle's receipt, or
- ask Paddle directly at [paddle.net](https://paddle.net).

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, the
Merchant of Record for our orders, so Paddle makes the refund. It goes
back to the card or account you paid with, usually within 5 to 10
working days.

## What happens to the licence

When a purchase is refunded, its licence key is cancelled and ITAM goes
back to the free version (up to 100 assets). **Nothing is deleted:** all
your data stays viewable and exportable.

## After 30 days

After 30 days you can still cancel at any time so that the subscription
does not renew; the key then runs until its end date. Your rights under
UK consumer law are not affected.

## Other CJW Apps products

The Microsoft Store version of ITAM and the book apps are sold by
Microsoft and Google, and their refund policies apply: ask Microsoft
through the order history in your Microsoft account, or Google through
your Google Play order history.

## Demo keys

Demo keys are free, so there is nothing to refund. Try the edition you
want for 30 days before you buy.
