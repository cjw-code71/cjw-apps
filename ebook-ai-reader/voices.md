# My voices

eBook AI Reader (Pro) can read English books in **six voices that come with
the app**: three female and three male, with UK, US and Australian accents.
They run entirely on your phone or PC, with no internet needed once the
voice model is downloaded.

## Good to know

- English books only. Books in other languages use your device's voice or
  the natural voice.
- Works best on a fast device (a phone with 8 GB of memory or more). If
  reading starts to pause between sentences, the app offers to switch back
  to a device voice.
- You can also add your own voices, made with a voice-making app of ours.
  Only use a voice you own or have permission to use.

The voices use Pocket TTS by Kyutai (voice model licensed
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)).
