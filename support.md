# Support

Questions, problems or ideas for **Audiobook Library**, **eBook AI Reader**
or **eBook Library**: email
[CJW-Apps@outlook.com](mailto:CJW-Apps@outlook.com). Please say which app,
whether you use Android or Windows, and what happened.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. The apps have no accounts with us. To use OneDrive or Google Drive you
sign in to Microsoft or Google on their own pages.

**Where is my library kept?**
On your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. Books in your cloud
storage stay there.

**How do I stop the app using my OneDrive or Google Drive?**
Disconnect it in the app (Sources). You can also remove the app's access
in your Microsoft or Google account settings.

**Will the app change my files?**
Only when you ask: with Update files / Update original file (Pro) you can
choose to save a book's details back into its file.

**How do I get Pro?**
Pro is a one-off purchase in Google Play or the Microsoft Store, from the
app's Settings. Each app has its own Pro.

**I bought Pro and reinstalled the app.**
Sign in to the same Google or Microsoft account you bought it with: the
store gives the purchase back to the app.

**A book won't open.**
Kindle books with DRM can't be opened. For other books, please email us
the file type and what you see.
