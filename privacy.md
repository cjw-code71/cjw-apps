# CJW Apps — Privacy Policy

*Last updated 17 October 2026*

This policy covers the apps published by CJW Apps:
[Audiobook Library](audiobook-library/), [eBook AI Reader](ebook-ai-reader/)
and [eBook Library](ebook-library/), on Android and Windows. Each app also
has its own page with the details for that app:
[Audiobook Library](audiobook-library/privacy.html),
[eBook AI Reader](ebook-ai-reader/privacy.html),
[eBook Library](ebook-library/privacy.html).

In short: **we don't collect anything.** There is no account with us, no
analytics, no advertising and no server of ours that your data goes to.

## What stays on your device

Everything the apps keep is stored only on your phone or PC: your library
(books and audiobooks, their covers, details and chapters), reading and
listening positions, bookmarks and notes, your settings, anything you
download, sign-in tokens for OneDrive and Google Drive and passwords for
catalogues you add (in your system's secure storage), and a small
diagnostic log that never contains tokens or passwords. Uninstalling an app
removes all of it.

## Your cloud storage (OneDrive, Google Drive)

The apps sign in to Microsoft and Google as **CJW Apps**. You sign in on
Microsoft's or Google's own page; the apps never see your password. They
then:

- **read** the files and folders you choose, to add books and audiobooks
  to your library, show their covers and details, and play or open them;
- **write** to your files **only when you ask**, when you choose to save a
  book's details (and, for audiobooks, its cover and chapters) back into its
  original file.

Your files go straight between your device and Microsoft or Google; they
never pass through a server of ours. The apps don't use your files, or any
data from them, for anything else: not for advertising, not to build
profiles, and they never share or sell it. Microsoft's and Google's own
privacy policies apply to your storage with them. You can disconnect an
account in an app at any time, and remove CJW Apps' access in your
Microsoft or Google account settings.

The apps' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data
Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Services the apps contact

To do what you ask, the apps make ordinary web requests to these services.
They receive what any website receives (such as your IP address) and the
search words or book you asked about, never account or personal
information from the apps.

| Service | App | When |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft (OneDrive), Google (Google Drive) | All | When you connect them and use your books there |
| LibriVox, via the Internet Archive | Audiobook Library | Free audiobooks |
| Audible and Audnexus, Apple Books search, Google Books | Audiobook Library | Match book |
| Project Gutenberg and Gutendex, Wikisource, the Internet Archive | eBook AI Reader, eBook Library | Free books |
| OPDS catalogues you add | eBook AI Reader, eBook Library | When you open them; your user name and password go only to that catalogue |
| Apple Books search, Open Library | eBook AI Reader, eBook Library | Find details online |
| Hugging Face | eBook AI Reader | Once, when you download the natural voice |

## Purchases

Pro is a one-off purchase in each app, made through Google Play or the
Microsoft Store. The store handles the payment; we never see your payment
details. The apps only learn whether Pro is unlocked.

## Children

The apps are not directed at children and collect no information from
anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
