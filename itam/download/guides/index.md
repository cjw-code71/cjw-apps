# <img class="title-icon app-icon" src="../../../assets/icons/itam.png?v=2" alt="" width="60" height="60">ITAM Download version: guides

The Download version has its own guides. They are published with the
Download version and will cover:

- installing ITAM on one PC, or as a Windows service on a server;
- setting up ITAM the first time, for one organisation, one school or a
  trust of schools;
- licence keys: trials, editions, upgrades and what happens when a key
  ends;
- schools and trusts: buildings and rooms, staff who see only their own
  school, transfers, and schools joining and leaving;
- network scans, Microsoft Intune and Google Workspace, backups and the
  network ports to open in a firewall.

Until then, the [Microsoft Store version guides](../../guides/) show how
ITAM is used day to day: most of it works the same in both versions.

To try the Download version now, or to join the schools pilot, [contact
us](../../../contact.html).
