<h2 id="install">1. Install the app</h2>

<span class="os">Windows</span> Get **Audiobook Library Player** from the
[Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NTCNMVBPCNG) and select
**Get** (or **Install**). When it's installed, open it from the Start menu.
You need Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).

<span class="os">Android</span> Audiobook Library is coming soon to Google
Play. When it's there, search Google Play for **Audiobook Library Player**
and tap **Install**. You need Android 7.0 or later. It works on phones,
tablets and Chromebooks.

The app is free. There's no account to create and no adverts. A few extra
features are in [Pro](#pro), a one-off purchase.

<h2 id="tour">2. A quick tour</h2>

The app has five tabs. On a PC or tablet they're down the left-hand side; on
a phone they're along the bottom of the screen.

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Continue listening, series in progress and recently added books |
| **Library** | All your books, with search, filters and sorting |
| **Sources** | Where your books come from: folders, OneDrive, Google Drive, LibriVox and downloads |
| **Bookmarks** | Your bookmarks and notes |
| **Settings** | Theme, playback options, Pro, and the in-app guide |

While a book is playing, a bar at the bottom of every tab shows it. Tap that
bar to go back to the player.

<h2 id="add">3. Add your audiobooks</h2>

Tap **+** on Home, or go to the **Sources** tab:

- **Add audiobooks** picks one or more audio files.
- **Add folder** takes a whole folder, with all its subfolders. This is the
  easiest way to add a big collection.

<span class="os">Android</span> **Add folder from another app** adds a folder
from pCloud, an SD card or a USB drive: anything that appears in Android's
file picker.

The app sorts your files into books for you:

- Each **M4B** file is one book.
- A folder of **MP3** files is one book.
- **CD1**, **CD2** (or Disc 1, Disc 2) folders are joined into one book.
- The author and series are taken from the folder names if the files don't
  have them.

Covers, titles, authors, narrators and chapters are read from your files in
the background, so a big library fills in over a few minutes. On Android, a
notification shows the progress.

**Added or renamed some books?** On the Sources tab, open the folder's menu
and choose **Rescan**. It finds new, changed and removed books. **Rescan all
folders** does every folder at once.

<h2 id="cloud">4. OneDrive and Google Drive</h2>

1. On the **Sources** tab, tap the **OneDrive** or **Google Drive** card.
2. Sign in on Microsoft's or Google's own page. The app never sees your
   password.
3. Choose the folder where you keep your audiobooks.

Your books then **stream**: they start playing straight away, without
downloading the whole book first. Covers and chapters are read without
downloading the audio. Streaming needs an internet connection; see
[Listening offline](#offline).

To use a different folder, choose **Change folder**. To stop using the
account, choose **Disconnect**. If you're asked to sign in again, use **Sign
in again** on the card: your library is kept.

<h2 id="free">5. Free audiobooks</h2>

Go to **Sources › LibriVox** to browse and search over 20,000 classic books
read by LibriVox volunteers. They're free and out of copyright. Tap a book,
then play it. It streams like any other book and appears in your library.

<h2 id="find">6. Find a book</h2>

- **Home** shows what you're listening to, series in progress and recently
  added books.
- **Library**: search, filter by author, series, genre, source or listening
  status, sort, and switch between a grid of covers and a list.

For more options on a book, **right-click** it <span class="os">Windows</span>
or **press and hold** it <span class="os">Android</span>. The menu has: other
books by the author or in the series, mark as finished, start again,
download for offline (Pro), and remove from the library.

<h2 id="listen">7. Listen</h2>

Tap a book to open the player, then press **Play**.

- **The seek bar** covers the current chapter, so it's easy to move around
  in it. The time left in the chapter and in the book are shown too.
- **Chapters** lists every chapter; tap one to jump to it.
- **The skip buttons** go back and forward a few seconds. You can change how
  far in [Settings](#settings).
- **Back to …**: after a big jump, this button takes you back to where you
  were.
- **Playback speed** goes from 0.75× to 3×, and is remembered for each book.
- **Smart rewind** steps back a few seconds when you come back after a pause,
  so you don't lose the thread.
- **More** (⋮): mark the book as finished, or start it again.

<h2 id="sleep">8. Sleep timer and bookmarks</h2>

**Sleep timer:** tap the moon button in the player. Choose a number of
minutes, or **End of chapter**. The sound fades out gently at the end. To
cancel it, tap the moon again and choose **Turn off**.

<span class="os">Android</span> Shake the phone near the end, or just after
it stops, for 10 more minutes.

**Bookmarks:** tap **Add bookmark** at the top of the player.
<span class="os">Android</span> You can also add one with the bookmark button
on the lock screen, without unlocking your phone.

The **Bookmarks** tab lists them all. Tap one to play from there, or add a
note to it.

<h2 id="controls">9. Lock screen, car and media keys</h2>

<span class="os">Android</span>

- Controls appear on the lock screen and in the notification. Headphone
  buttons work too.
- **Android Auto:** browse Continue listening, Recently added, Authors and
  Series in the car, or say *"Hey Google, play &lt;title&gt; on Audiobook
  Library"*. Steering-wheel buttons play, pause and skip.

<span class="os">Windows</span>

- The play/pause and next/previous keys on your keyboard work, even when the
  app isn't in front.
- Windows shows the book in its media controls (the volume pop-up, or Quick
  Settings on Windows 11).

<h2 id="offline">10. Listening offline</h2>

- Books from **folders on your device** always play offline.
- Books from **OneDrive, Google Drive, LibriVox** and other apps' folders
  stream, so they need the internet.
- With **Pro**, you can download those books to play offline, for example
  before a flight: choose **Download for offline** on the book's page or in
  its menu. **Sources › Downloads** lists your downloaded books.

<h2 id="pro">11. Pro</h2>

Pro is a **one-off purchase**, not a subscription. Buy it in **Settings ›
Audiobook Library Pro**. It's bought through the Microsoft Store on Windows
and Google Play on Android, so each one is bought separately.

- **Download for offline:** keep books from any source on your device.
- **Match book** (in a book's menu): finds the narrator, series,
  description, cover and chapters online.
- **Edit details:** change a book's title, author, series, cover and more by
  hand.
- **Update files:** save the details, cover and chapters into your audio
  files, and upload them over the originals in your cloud storage if you
  choose.

**New PC or phone?** Open **Settings › Audiobook Library Pro** and choose
**Restore purchase**, signed in to the same Microsoft or Google account you
bought it with.

Free LibriVox books are kept as they came: their details and files can't be
changed.

<h2 id="settings">12. Settings</h2>

- **Appearance:** light, dark or the same as your device, and an accent
  colour (or pick your own).
- **Playback:** how far the skip buttons go, and smart rewind.
- **Sleep timer:** fade out, go back a minute when it stops (in case you
  dozed off), and <span class="os">Android</span> shake to extend.
- **Sound** <span class="os">Android</span>: skip silence trims gaps in the
  narration; volume boost helps with quiet recordings or noisy places.
- **How to use Audiobook Library:** a short version of this guide, inside
  the app.

<h2 id="help">13. Tips and help</h2>

- **Removing a book or folder from the library never deletes your files.**
- A book is missing or in the wrong place? **Rescan** its folder on the
  Sources tab. Check that each book is in its own folder, or is a single M4B
  file.
- A cloud book won't play? Check your internet connection, then try again.
  If you're asked, choose **Sign in again** on the Sources tab.
- Still stuck? See [Support](../support.html), or email
  [CJW-Apps@outlook.com](mailto:CJW-Apps@outlook.com) with your device and
  what happened. A screenshot helps.
