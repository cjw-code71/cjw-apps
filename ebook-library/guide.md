works the same way on both. Where something is different, it's marked

<h2 id="install">1. Install the app</h2>

<span class="os">Windows</span> Get **eBook Library Bookshelf** from the
[Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9P23H7S4TF02) and select
**Get** (or **Install**). When it's installed, open it from the Start menu.
You need Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).

<span class="os">Android</span> eBook Library is coming soon to Google Play.
When it's there, search Google Play for **eBook Library Bookshelf** and tap
**Install**. You need Android 7.0 or later. It works on phones, tablets and
Chromebooks.

The app is free. There's no account to create and no adverts. A few extra
features are in [Pro](#pro), a one-off purchase.

<h2 id="tour">2. A quick tour</h2>

The app has five tabs. On a PC or tablet they're down the left-hand side; on
a phone they're along the bottom of the screen.

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Continue reading, series in progress and recently added books |
| **Library** | All your books, with search, filters and sorting |
| **Sources** | Where your books come from: folders, OneDrive, Google Drive and free books |
| **Bookmarks** | Your bookmarks and notes |
| **Settings** | Theme, Pro, and the in-app guide |

<h2 id="add">3. Add your ebooks</h2>

Tap **+** on Home or in the Library, or go to the **Sources** tab:

- **Add books** picks one or more book files.
- **Add folder** brings in every book in a folder and its subfolders, for
  example a whole series, in order.

<span class="os">Android</span> **Add folder from another app** adds a folder
from pCloud, an SD card or a USB drive.

**Which books work?** ePub, Kindle (MOBI, AZW, AZW3, without DRM), PDF, FB2,
Word (DOCX), RTF, text and HTML.

**A book you've just downloaded:**

- <span class="os">Windows</span> Drag the file onto the app's window, or
  right-click it and choose **Open with › eBook Library**.
- <span class="os">Android</span> Open it from your browser's downloads or
  the Files app and choose **eBook Library**, or share it to the app.

Folders you add are kept on the **Sources** tab. From a folder's menu:

- **Rescan** finds new, changed and removed books. **Rescan all folders**
  does every folder at once.
- **Remove from library** removes the books that came from that folder. Your
  files are never touched.

<h2 id="cloud">4. OneDrive and Google Drive</h2>

1. Tap **+**, or go to the **Sources** tab, and choose **OneDrive** or
   **Google Drive**.
2. Sign in on Microsoft's or Google's own page. The app never sees your
   password.
3. Browse your folders and tap books to add them, or choose **Add this
   folder**.

The books are copied into the app, so you can read them offline.

<h2 id="free">5. Free books</h2>

Go to **Sources › Free books**:

- **Project Gutenberg, Wikisource and the Internet Archive:** tens of
  thousands of free classics. Tap a book to add it.
- **Archive of Our Own:** opens in your browser. Tap **Entire Work**, then
  **Download › EPUB**, and open the file with eBook Library.
- **Your catalogues:** add an OPDS address, such as your Calibre library.

<h2 id="find">6. Find a book</h2>

- **Home** shows what you're reading, series in progress and recently added
  books.
- **Library**: search, filter by author, series, genre or reading status,
  sort, and switch between a grid of covers and a list.

For more options on a book, **right-click** it <span class="os">Windows</span>
or **press and hold** it <span class="os">Android</span>. The menu has: its
details, edit details, other books by the author or in its series, mark as
finished or unread, and remove from the library.

<h2 id="read">7. Read</h2>

Tap a book to open it. A new book starts at the Prologue or Chapter 1, and
every book reopens where you left off.

- **Scroll** to read. **Contents** lists the chapters. The buttons at the
  bottom go to the previous or next chapter.
- **Read in pages instead:** tap the **T** button at the top, then
  **Reading › Pages**.
- **Text size and line spacing:** also under the **T** button.
- **Copy text:** press and hold (or drag over) the text to select it.

**Turning pages:**

- <span class="os">Windows</span> Click the left or right side of the page,
  use the mouse wheel, the ← → arrow keys, or Page Up / Page Down. Space
  goes to the next screen and Shift + Space goes back; Ctrl + ← → moves a
  chapter.
- <span class="os">Android</span> Swipe, tap the left or right side of the
  page, or use the volume keys (switch this on under the **T** button).

Turning the page on from the last page of a chapter goes to the next
chapter.

<h2 id="bookmarks">8. Bookmarks</h2>

Tap **Add bookmark** at the top of the reader to save your place, with a
note if you like.

The **Bookmarks** tab lists them all. Tap one to read from there, or add and
edit notes. Removing a book also removes its bookmarks.

<h2 id="details">9. Book details</h2>

**Book details** (in the book's menu, or ⋮ in the reader) shows the cover,
author, series, genres, description, publisher, date, language and ISBN.
Tap an author, series or genre to see all its books in the Library.

With [Pro](#pro) you can also:

- **Edit details:** change any of them, including the cover (choose an image
  file). Authors, series and genres already in your library are suggested as
  you type.
- **Find details online:** searches Apple Books and Open Library. Pick the
  right book, tick the details you want (cover, description, genres, date
  and more), and choose **Apply**.
- **Update original file:** write the details into your own book file, for
  example on your pCloud drive. Or tick **Also update the original file**
  when you save. Kindle, PDF and some other formats can't hold the details,
  so you choose between an ePub copy next to the file or an ePub in its
  place. The first time, a backup of the file is kept.

Your changes are kept in the library; the book file itself isn't changed
unless you ask. **Export updated ePub** (free, in the book's menu) saves a
copy with the details and cover written in, for other apps and devices.

<h2 id="several">10. Several books at once</h2>

In the Library, right-click <span class="os">Windows</span> or press and hold
<span class="os">Android</span> a book and choose **Select**, then tap more
books.

<span class="os">Windows</span> Ctrl-click adds a book, Shift-click selects
a range, and Ctrl + A selects every book shown.

**Select all shown** takes every book the search or filters show, such as
one series or author. Then:

- **Edit together** (Pro): set or clear the author, series (numbered in the
  Library's order), genres, publisher or language for all of them.
- **Find details online** (Pro): each book is searched in turn. By default
  only missing details are filled in.
- **⋮** in the selection bar: update the original files, mark as finished or
  unread, or remove.

<h2 id="pro">11. Pro</h2>

Pro is a **one-off purchase**, not a subscription. Buy it in **Settings ›
eBook Library Pro**. It's bought through the Microsoft Store on Windows and
Google Play on Android, so each one is bought separately.

- **Edit details:** change a book's details and cover.
- **Find details online,** for one book or many.
- **Edit together:** change several books at once.
- **Update original files:** save the details back into your own book files.

Reading, Book details and Export updated ePub are free.

**New PC or phone?** Open **Settings › eBook Library Pro** and choose
**Restore purchase**, signed in to the same Microsoft or Google account you
bought it with.

<h2 id="help">12. Tips and help</h2>

- **Books are copied into the app when you add them. Removing a book from the
  library never deletes your own file.**
- Series come from the ebook itself, a "Volume 3" style title, or the folder
  the book was added from.
- Theme (light, dark or the same as your device) and accent colour are in
  **Settings**. **Settings › How to use eBook Library** is a short version
  of this guide, inside the app.
- A Kindle book won't open? Books with DRM (most books bought from Amazon)
  can't be opened by any app other than Kindle.
- Still stuck? See [Support](../support.html), or email
  [CJW-Apps@outlook.com](mailto:CJW-Apps@outlook.com) with your device and
  what happened. A screenshot helps.
