CJW-Apps ITAM — Documentation

Version 2.9.0

CJW-Apps ITAM is an IT asset register for Windows. It keeps your PCs, servers, network devices, databases and software licences in one place, finds devices on your network without installing anything on them, and brings in the devices Microsoft Intune or Google Workspace already manage. All data stays on your own PC.

Words used in these guides

Word Meaning
Asset / CI (configuration item) One item in the inventory, such as a server, laptop, switch or database
CI type The kind of asset (Server, Laptop, Database...). Each type belongs to a dashboard group
Roles Viewer looks and searches; Editor also adds, changes, imports and exports; Administrator also changes settings. The built-in admin account is the Owner
Staged device A device a network scan found that is not in the inventory yet, or differs from it. It waits for review
Saved login A user name and password, or SNMP community, ITAM uses to read details during a scan. Stored encrypted
Imported By Where an asset came from (Intune, Google Workspace, a network scan or a CSV file), who imported it and when
