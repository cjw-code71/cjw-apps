<h2 id="install">1. Install the app</h2>

<span class="os">Windows</span> Get **eBook AI Reader** from the
[Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NWL094R78JQ) and select
**Get** (or **Install**). When it's installed, open it from the Start menu.
You need Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).

<span class="os">Android</span> eBook AI Reader is coming soon to Google
Play. When it's there, search Google Play for **eBook AI Reader** and tap
**Install**. You need Android 7.0 or later. It works on phones, tablets and
Chromebooks.

The app is free. There's no account to create and no adverts. The natural
voices and a few other extras are in [Pro](#pro), a one-off purchase.

<h2 id="tour">2. A quick tour</h2>

The app has five tabs. On a PC or tablet they're down the left-hand side; on
a phone they're along the bottom of the screen.

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Continue reading, series in progress and recently added books |
| **Library** | All your books, with search, filters and sorting |
| **Sources** | Where your books come from: folders, OneDrive, Google Drive and free books |
| **Bookmarks** | Your bookmarks and notes |
| **Settings** | Reading aloud, voices, theme, Pro, and the in-app guide |

<h2 id="add">3. Add your ebooks</h2>

Go to the **Sources** tab, or tap **+** in the Library:

- **Add books** picks one or more book files.
- **Add folder** brings in every book in a folder and its subfolders, for
  example a whole series, in order.

<span class="os">Android</span> **Add folder from another app** adds a folder
from pCloud, an SD card or a USB drive.

**Which books work?** ePub, Kindle (MOBI, AZW, AZW3, without DRM), PDF, FB2,
Word (DOCX), RTF, text and HTML.

**A book you've just downloaded:**

- <span class="os">Windows</span> Drag the file onto the app's window, or
  right-click it and choose **Open with › eBook AI Reader**.
- <span class="os">Android</span> Open it from your browser's downloads or
  the Files app and choose **eBook AI Reader**, or share it to the app.

Folders you add are kept on the **Sources** tab. From a folder's menu:

- **Rescan** finds new, changed and removed books.
- **Remove from library** removes the books that came from that folder. Your
  files are never touched.

<h2 id="cloud">4. OneDrive and Google Drive</h2>

1. On the **Sources** tab, tap the **OneDrive** or **Google Drive** card.
2. Sign in on Microsoft's or Google's own page. The app never sees your
   password. (Choose **Cancel** if you change your mind.)
3. Browse your folders and tap books to add them, or choose **Add this
   folder**.

The books are copied into the app, so you can read them offline.

<h2 id="free">5. Free books</h2>

Go to **Sources › Free books**:

- **Project Gutenberg, Wikisource and the Internet Archive:** tens of
  thousands of free classics. Tap a book to add it.
- **Archive of Our Own:** opens in your browser. Tap **Entire Work**, then
  **Download › EPUB**, and open the file with eBook AI Reader.
- **Your catalogues:** add an OPDS address, such as your Calibre library.

<h2 id="find">6. Find a book</h2>

- **Home** shows what you're reading, series in progress and recently added
  books.
- **Library**: search, filter by author, series or reading status, sort,
  and switch between a grid of covers and a list.

For more options on a book, **right-click** it <span class="os">Windows</span>
or **press and hold** it <span class="os">Android</span>. The menu has: other
books by the author or in its series, set its series (Pro), mark as finished
or unread, and remove from the library.

<h2 id="read">7. Read and listen</h2>

Tap a book to open it. A new book starts at the Prologue or Chapter 1, and
every book reopens where you left off.

- **Press Play** to have the book read aloud. The sentence being read is
  highlighted, and the page follows along.
- **Tap any sentence** to read from there.
- **The buttons** go to the previous or next sentence and chapter.
  **Contents** lists the chapters.
- **Voice and text** (the sliders button): the voice, speed, pitch, text
  size and spacing, and whether to scroll or read in pages.

<span class="os">Windows</span> Keyboard shortcuts: Space plays or pauses,
← → move a sentence, and Ctrl + ← → move a chapter.

Reading aloud carries on in the background, so you can use other apps or
<span class="os">Android</span> turn the screen off.

<h2 id="voices">8. Choose a voice</h2>

Open **Voice and text** (the sliders button) in the reader. There are three
kinds of voice:

- **Device voices** (free): the voices built into Windows or Android. To get
  more, add them in your device's settings (on Windows: **Settings › Time &
  language › Speech**; on Android: **Settings › Text-to-speech output**),
  then choose **Refresh voices**.
- **The natural voice** (Pro): much more natural reading that works
  offline. In **Settings**, find **Natural voice (Supertonic)** and choose
  **Download** (about 146 MB, once).
- **My voices** (Pro): English books read in six included voices (UK, US and
  Australian, female and male), or in voices you made with the Voice Studio
  app. In **Settings**, find **My voices** and choose **Download** (about
  125 MB, once). [Hear the six voices](voices.html).

The natural voices run on your device, so they need a reasonably fast PC or
phone. My voices can take up to 3 seconds to start, and works best on a
phone with 8 GB of memory or more. If reading starts to pause between
sentences, the app offers to switch back to a device voice.

<h2 id="sleep">9. Sleep timer and bookmarks</h2>

**Sleep timer:** tap the moon button in the reader. Choose a number of
minutes, or **End of chapter**. To cancel it, tap the moon again and choose
**Turn off**.

<span class="os">Android</span> Shake the phone in the last minute, or just
after it stops, for 10 more minutes.

In **Settings › Reading aloud** you can choose: smart rewind after a pause,
whether headset and car buttons move by sentence, and the sleep timer's
fade-out and going back a paragraph when it stops (in case you dozed off).

**Bookmarks:** tap **Add bookmark** at the top of the reader. It saves the
sentence being read, with a note if you like.
<span class="os">Android</span> The bookmark button on the lock screen saves
one with a single tap.

The **Bookmarks** tab lists them all. Tap one to read from there, or add and
edit notes.

<h2 id="controls">10. Lock screen, car and media keys</h2>

<span class="os">Android</span>

- Controls appear on the lock screen and in the notification. Headphone
  buttons work too.
- **Android Auto:** browse Continue reading, Recently added, Library,
  Authors and Series in the car, or say *"Hey Google, read &lt;title&gt; on
  eBook AI Reader"*. Books are read aloud; the text isn't shown in the car.

<span class="os">Windows</span>

- The play/pause and next/previous keys on your keyboard work, even when the
  app isn't in front.
- Windows shows the book in its media controls (the volume pop-up, or Quick
  Settings on Windows 11).

<h2 id="pro">11. Pro</h2>

Pro is a **one-off purchase**, not a subscription. Buy it in **Settings ›
eBook AI Reader Pro**. It's bought through the Microsoft Store on Windows and
Google Play on Android, so each one is bought separately.

- **The natural voice:** high-quality reading aloud, offline.
- **My voices:** English books read in six included voices, or in your own,
  offline.
- **Edit details and Set series:** change a book's details and cover by
  hand.
- **Find details online:** covers, descriptions and series. Select several
  books in the Library to edit them together or find details for them all.
- **Update original files:** save the details back into your own book files.

**New PC or phone?** Open **Settings › eBook AI Reader Pro** and choose
**Restore purchase**, signed in to the same Microsoft or Google account you
bought it with.

Free books are kept as they came: their details can't be changed.

<h2 id="help">12. Tips and help</h2>

- **Books are copied into the app when you add them. Removing a book from the
  library never deletes your own file.**
- Series come from the ebook itself, a "Volume 3" style title, or the folder
  the book was added from. Fix one with **Set series** in the book's menu
  (Pro).
- Theme (light, dark or the same as your device) and accent colour are in
  **Settings**. **Settings › How to use eBook AI Reader** is a short version
  of this guide, inside the app.
- A Kindle book won't open? Books with DRM (most books bought from Amazon)
  can't be opened by any app other than Kindle.
- Still stuck? See [Support](../support.html), or email
  [CJW-Apps@outlook.com](mailto:CJW-Apps@outlook.com) with your device and
  what happened. A screenshot helps.
