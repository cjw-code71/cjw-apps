# Support

Questions, problems or ideas for any CJW App: email
[CJW-Apps@outlook.com](mailto:CJW-Apps@outlook.com). Please say which app
and what happened. The [Contact](contact.html) page lists what helps us
answer quickly.

- [Entertainment apps](#entertainment-apps): Audiobook Library, eBook AI
  Reader and eBook Library
- [CJW-Apps ITAM](#cjw-apps-itam)

## Entertainment apps

For **Audiobook Library**, **eBook AI Reader** and **eBook Library**.
Please say which app and whether you use Android or Windows.

**Do I need an account?**
No. The apps have no accounts with us. To use OneDrive or Google Drive you
sign in to Microsoft or Google on their own pages.

**Where is my library kept?**
On your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. Books in your cloud
storage stay there.

**How do I stop the app using my OneDrive or Google Drive?**
Disconnect it in the app (Sources). You can also remove the app's access
in your Microsoft or Google account settings.

**Will the app change my files?**
Only when you ask: with Update files / Update original file (Pro) you can
choose to save a book's details back into its file.

**How do I get Pro?**
Pro is a one-off purchase in Google Play or the Microsoft Store, from the
app's Settings. Each app has its own Pro.

**I bought Pro and reinstalled the app.**
Sign in to the same Google or Microsoft account you bought it with: the
store gives the purchase back to the app.

**A book won't open.**
Kindle books with DRM can't be opened. For other books, please email us
the file type and what you see.

## CJW-Apps ITAM

**[Read the ITAM guides](itam/guides/)**: Getting Started, Installation &
Configuration (including the network ports to open in a firewall), the
User Guide and Troubleshooting. These are the questions asked most.

**Do I need an account with CJW Apps?**
No. ITAM runs on your own PC and has its own sign-in. The first time, sign
in as `admin` with the password shown in the Quick start guide; ITAM then
asks you to choose your own.

**Where is my inventory kept?**
On the PC that runs ITAM. Nothing is sent to us. Make backups in
**Settings → Backups & Storage**, to a folder of your choice.

**What does the free version include?**
Up to 100 assets, with network discovery and CSV imports. Software
licences and decommissioned assets are not counted. ITAM Pro removes the
limit and adds Microsoft Intune and Google Workspace device imports.

**How do I get Pro, and how do I cancel it?**
In the Microsoft Store copy, the built-in `admin` account presses
**Upgrade to Pro** (in the banner or in **About**). Pro has a 30-day free
trial. To cancel, press **Cancel Pro...** in About: it opens your Microsoft
account's subscriptions page. If Pro ends, nothing is deleted or locked;
only adding new assets pauses.

**It says Pro (offline), or that Pro could not be checked.**
ITAM checks Pro with the Microsoft Store. Connect the PC to the internet,
then press **Recover purchase** in About.

**Will I lose my data if I uninstall?**
With the Microsoft Store copy, yes: Windows removes the app's storage.
Make a full backup to another folder first, and restore it after
reinstalling. Updates keep your data.

**Can my colleagues use it?**
Yes. An administrator turns on network sharing in **Settings → Server &
Access**; colleagues then open it in their browser over HTTPS and sign in
with the role you give them (viewer, editor or administrator).

**I closed the window. Is ITAM still running?**
Yes. Click the ITAM icon near the clock to open it again, or right-click
it and choose **Quit ITAM**.

**Something else went wrong.**
Email us what you did and what you saw. The file `itam.log` in ITAM's data
folder helps; it never contains passwords or keys.
