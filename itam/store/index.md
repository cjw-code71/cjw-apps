# <img class="title-icon app-icon plain" src="../../assets/icons/itam.png" alt="" width="60" height="60">ITAM: Microsoft Store version

For starters and small businesses. Install it from the Microsoft Store and
start straight away. Updates come automatically through the Store.

**Guides:** [Microsoft Store version guides](../guides/)

## Editions and prices

<div class="table-scroll" markdown="1">

| Edition | What you get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Up to 100 assets, with network discovery, CSV imports, licences, alerts, roles, the Finance page and backups | Free |
| **ITAM Pro** | No asset limit, plus Microsoft Intune and Google Workspace device imports, on demand or on a schedule | £44.99 a month or £454.99 a year, with a 30-day free trial |

</div>

- Software licences and decommissioned assets do not count towards the
  100 assets.
- You pay for ITAM Pro through your Microsoft account. Cancel any time
  there.
- If Pro ends, nothing is deleted or locked. Only adding new assets
  pauses.
